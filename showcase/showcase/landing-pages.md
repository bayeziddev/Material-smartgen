# Landing Pages

Landing pages focus on one objective: converting visitors into customers, subscribers, or leads.

## Common Sections

- Hero banner
- Benefits
- Features
- Pricing
- Testimonials
- FAQs
- Call-to-action
- Contact form

## Conversion Strategies

- Strong headlines
- Simple layouts
- Trust indicators
- Fast loading
- Mobile-first design
- Clear buttons

These examples demonstrate proven layouts suitable for SaaS products, agencies, online courses, and digital services.
