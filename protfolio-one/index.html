01 / The point of view

Digital work should feel like an advantage, not another thing to manage.

Most digital work fails quietly: a site nobody maintains, an audit that goes stale, a dashboard only the agency knows how to open. I build the opposite — clear systems with a human hand in the details.

That means fewer, deeper things: a tools platform with real utility, a lead collector that keeps only verified data, a storefront that works on ordinary mobile data, and open-source material anyone can reskin.
